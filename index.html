Game Burrow privacy policy

Updated 2026-11-01

Game Burrow is a card game app for children, with War, Go Fish, Crazy Eights and Solitaire against friendly forest animals. This page explains what it does with your information. The short answer: nothing. It collects none.

What we collect

Game Burrow collects no personal information. We do not ask for a name, an email address, an age, a location or anything else about you or your child.

No ads, purchases, accounts or internet

  • There are no ads.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • There are no accounts or sign-ins.
  • The app does not use the internet. It does not ask for network access and sends nothing anywhere.
  • There are no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party services.

Game progress

Games in progress, your wins and settings (such as the sound switch, card deck and game speed) are saved only on the device. They never leave it. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Children

Game Burrow is made for children, including young ones. Because it collects nothing and connects to nothing, there is no child data to share or protect beyond the device itself.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
